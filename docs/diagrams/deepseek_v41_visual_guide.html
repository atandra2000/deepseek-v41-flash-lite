Model

A 24-block multimodal decoder

All blocks have SWA-128 and MoE. L0–1 are SWA-only. Later blocks add the configured global path. ViT alignment fills image slots; mHC carries two residual streams. Engram enters layers 1 and 14, while DSpark is a separate optional draft path.

Data

Offline preparation and verified packing

Checksummed local sources become uint16 shards, a SQLite document index and separate image patches. Duplicate identities or content abort preparation instead of being silently removed. The manifest is written only on success. Content hashes determine split and order, not exact corpus quotas.

Packing allocates int64 prediction tensors. Production budgets are gates, not an inventory of prepared data.

Training

Guarded updates, bounded recovery

CUDA enables BF16 autocast in this path. Token-normalized loss is accumulated before clipping and AdamW. Nonfinite loss, gradient norm, parameters or optimizer state trigger rollback, two skipped batches and halved learning rate, with at most three rollbacks.

Clipping more than five of the last 100 attempts fails without retry. Dataset exhaustion propagates StopIteration, including during retry skips. It does not reach the final checkpoint written after a normal target-step completion.

Distinctive mechanism

CED ownership and CSA2 selection

Each decoder projects layer 8 hidden states into its own global KV cache. L12 selects top-k from all reachable scores and builds a separate candidate pool. L16 and L20 mask their scores to that pool, while other decoder layers reuse the latest indices. Sparse selection does not evict full-length KV buffers.

Selection is not storage

An upper-bound illustration for the last decoder query, not a measured cache reduction or speedup. Partial blocks can reduce the pool's actual position count. L12 selects its own top-k from the full reachable stream and publishes the pool for later reindexers. Full-length KV buffers remain allocated.

Lite behavior takes precedence over early design prose

Encoder layers 2 and 8 publish compressed KV at ratio 2. Same-ratio encoder consumers reuse the latest cache. Decoder layers 12–23 instead project layer 8's final hidden state through their own CedProjection at ratio 1. They do not read the encoder cache directly. L12 owns the decoder index-key cache used by L16 and L20.

Sources: ced.py, indexer.py, csa2.py and transformer.py.
